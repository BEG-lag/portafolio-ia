04 · Featured · RAG

A retrieval engine over Mexican energy law.

Problem. Mexico's energy rules are scattered across the Official Gazette (DOF) and the websites of eight energy authorities, and the 2025 reform replaced much of the framework. Finding the instrument that actually applies, and knowing whether it still applies, takes hours.

What I built. A pipeline that collects, structures and indexes Mexican energy legislation so it can be queried in natural language. No LangChain or LlamaIndex: plain Python and SQL.

Scale (April 2026)

374 documents

11,112 chunks, all embedded

352 in force 22 repealed

  • CRE
  • SENER
  • CENACE
  • CNH
  • CENAGAS
  • CNE
  • ASEA
  • Congress

  1. Stage 1

    Collection

    Async scrapers over the Official Gazette and regulators' sites, with polite delays.

  2. Stage 2

    Extraction

    Text from PDFs with an OCR fallback for scanned documents.

  3. Stage 3

    Chunking

    Splits text along the hierarchy of Mexican legal instruments (title, chapter, article) instead of fixed sizes.

  4. Stage 4

    Storage

    PostgreSQL with pgvector: documents and chunks, validity flags and a validity note for rules that are formally in force but suspended in practice.

  5. Stage 5

    Embeddings

    Voyage AI voyage-law-2, built for legal text.

  6. Stage 6

    Search

    Hybrid ranking (vector similarity plus keyword, merged with reciprocal rank fusion), synonyms, statute detection and boosts.

  7. Stage 7

    Answers

    Claude answers natural-language questions over the retrieved chunks.

  8. Stage 8

    Quality

    A "golden" test set used as a regression guardrail (9 of 9 passing) and database audits for duplicates, validity and publication dates.

06 · Featured · n8n

A 20-step real-estate closing, automated with a hand on the brake.

Problem. A real-estate law firm in Baja California closes property purchases by foreign buyers through Mexican bank trusts. Each closing has 20 steps and depends on a notary, a trust bank, an appraiser, an escrow agent and the public registry. Most of the lawyers' time goes to chasing documents and answering "what's the status of my closing?".

  • 11can run almost on their own

Signing gate

No one books the closing until six conditions are met.

  1. Foreign-affairs permit granted
  2. Appraisal received
  3. Certificates still valid on the proposed date, not just today
  4. Deed draft approved by the trust bank
  5. Funds confirmed in escrow
  6. Final balance paid

6 of 6: the closing can now be booked

Anti-fraud · red alert

No workflow ever sends bank details, and any email about new wiring instructions raises a red alert and requires a verification call.

  • Document AI with human approval. It reads passports and certificates and compares the deed draft against the record.
  • Client data never lives on my infrastructure. The demo runs on fictitious data and production goes on the firm's own server.

Proposal delivered; decisions kept in a decision register; rollout planned in 4 phases over 10 to 13 weeks [ESTIMATE], starting with a pilot on one development. Expected savings: 50% to 70% of busywork hours per closing [ESTIMATE], to be measured in the diagnosis.

11 · Desktop companion

BeCa: my cat knows what Claude is doing.

BeCa is my cat turned into a sticker-style companion that lives in a small floating window on my Mac. It listens to Claude Code's lifecycle hooks, so I can tell at a glance what Claude is doing without looking at the terminal. Why BeCa? To bring a sense of humor to my day-to-day work with AI.

BeCa

clickClick it once and it waves; click again and it plays with Clawd for about four seconds.

It runs locally, uses no network and stores no content. The hooks are global, so BeCa works in every project.

  1. Claude Code starts a session

    SessionStart

    Waves twice, then rests

  2. Works on a request or runs a tool

    UserPromptSubmit PreToolUse PostToolUse

    Types in the air

  3. Edits or writes a file

    PostToolUse of Edit or Write

    Inspects the work, paw on chin

  4. Finishes its turn

    Stop

    Jumps four times and waves

  5. Waits for my permission or answer

    PermissionRequest Notification

    Asks with its paws together

  6. Hits an API error

    StopFailure

    Deflates once

  7. Closes the session

    SessionEnd

    Rests

How it was built: a native macOS app in Swift, made with Claude Code; the art was generated with Higgsfield from three photos of my cat and cut into animation strips with Python scripts; the sounds were extracted from AI-generated video clips.

14 · How I work with AI

Seven rules, none of them negotiable.

1/7

  1. Sources first.

    Official texts are downloaded into the project, and each data point carries its source and cut-off date.

  2. Uncertainty is labeled.

    Every deliverable marks [ESTIMATE], [ASSUMPTION] and [DATA GAP].

  3. People approve what matters.

    Signatures, money and legal judgment always go through a person.

  4. Reproducible deliverables.

    Markdown first, then PDF with pandoc and Typst; generator scripts rebuild the final files.

  5. Decisions are logged.

    Decision registers and GREEN, YELLOW and RED status lights.

  6. Test like a user.

    Simulated buyers, golden test sets, automated tests and checks against the live site.

  7. Validation is explicit.

    Studies state what must be confirmed with the competent authority and are not legal advice.
